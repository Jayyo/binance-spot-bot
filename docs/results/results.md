# Backtest results

Generated by `python scripts/readme_results.py`. Every number below comes from one run of that script.

## Conditions

- Data: Binance spot monthly klines, 49 archives, each SHA-256 matched Binance's checksum file
- Warm-up: 744 earlier bars feed the indicators; no trading, not in the metrics
- Strategy: RSI(14) < 30 enter, > 70 exit
- Sizing: 100% of bot equity per entry; long only, no leverage
- Stop: 3% below the entry fill, checked against each bar's low; fills at min(open, stop)
- Daily loss: entries pause for the UTC day once equity is 2% below its 00:00 value
- Entries: at most 10 per UTC day, 1 bar cooldown after an exit
- Execution: decision at a bar's close, market fill at the next bar's open
- Costs: fee 0.10% and slippage 0.05% on every fill, same for buy & hold
- Start / end: 10,000 USDT; any open position is sold at the last close
- Not modelled: exchange order filters, order book depth, fees paid in BNB
- Period: 2022-01 to 2025-12 (UTC), 1h bars, warm-up from 2021-12
- Missing bars: 2023-03-24 13:00 UTC (1 bar missing)
- Symbols: BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, picked as large and liquid, not as a market sample; all three survived and are correlated.
- "Before costs" reruns the same rules with zero fee and slippage.
